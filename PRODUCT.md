# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
- Families with children seeking comprehensive dental care
- Adults looking for cosmetic dentistry treatments (aligners, whitening, veneers)
- Professionals seeking high-quality, convenient dental services
- Patients needing flexible scheduling (after-hours appointments)

## Product Purpose
To showcase Complete Dental Clinic's services, build trust with potential patients, and enable easy appointment booking. Success means visitors understand the clinic's offerings, trust the quality of care, and book appointments.

## Positioning
Complete Dental Clinic differentiates through:
- Specialized 3D aligner technology
- Kid-friendly pediatric care environment
- Flexible after-hour appointments
- High-quality cosmetic treatments
- Budget-friendly pricing without compromising quality

## Operating Context
- Patients research dental options online before booking
- Parents look for child-friendly dental environments
- Professionals need convenient scheduling options
- Patients compare services, pricing, and reviews
- Appointment booking should be simple and quick

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Static website (no backend database)
- Appointment booking via form submission (not real-time scheduling)
- Contact form for inquiries
- Responsive design for mobile and desktop
- Must use authentic clinic photos (not AI-generated)
- Must avoid "AI slop" design patterns

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Complete Dental Clinic (Complete Dental Clinic)
- Logo: Available at `logo-1.jpg`
- Voice: Professional, caring, trustworthy, approachable
- Color Palette:
  - Primary: Teal/Cyan (#00A896) - modern health, cleanliness
  - Secondary: Forest Green (#0A5C36) - growth, care
  - Backgrounds: Pure White (#FFFFFF), Soft Light Gray (#F8F9FA)
  - Typography: Dark Charcoal (#1A1A1A) - high-contrast, professional
- Tagline: "happy smiles always!"
